Privacy Policy

NoAI does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

This extension operates entirely within the user's browser:

  • It uses declarativeNetRequest to modify Google Search URLs locally — no data leaves the browser.
  • It uses storage to save only the on/off preference locally on the user's device.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no external servers, no third-party code.
